# Home (Figma, Demandas): desktop + mobile

Mode: Operate. Visitor: a recruiter evaluating a portfolio piece who wants to place a food order. Deliverable: Figma design only (HTML withdrawn by user). Palette and layout family pinned by the user's references (mint-green scale, left nav + category picker + right-hand basket). Direction pinned by brief, so no concept roll was run.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One screen answers "what am I eating and where is my order": browse, basket, and live order status together. Refuses the generic food-app hero that pushes the menu below the fold.
OWN-WORLD: Committed mint green. Deep forest rail (#0D261E) carries the shell, electric mint (#19ECA1) is the single action color, pale mint page ground (#F3FCF8), white cards, 12-16px radii, soft green-tinted shadows. Barlow (DIN stand-in) for headings and prices, Figtree Light/Regular (Avenir stand-in) for body. Real food photography, authored line icons.
STORY: Visitor sees the four areas (Home, Search, Track order, Account), spots an order already in progress, picks a category, restaurant or dish, adds to basket, and checks out from the always-visible basket.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440: 216px dark rail left, main column with greeting + search + address, active-order strip, compact promo, then categories; 344px basket panel right with items, fees, total, mint Checkout button. Mobile 390: address + bell, search, active-order chip, category row, promo, restaurants, dishes, floating basket bar above a 4-tab bar.
FORM: Operate dashboard-style ordering shell, closest to the user's two references. seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
