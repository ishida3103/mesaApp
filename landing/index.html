From hungry to fed in four steps.

The same screens you see on the right are the app itself, designed for the phone in your hand.

Pick a place, then a dish.

Restaurants are listed near you. Open one and its menu is organized by category, with a photo, a description and a price on every item.

Build your basket.

Add items with one tap, change quantities, leave a note for the kitchen and see the total before you commit.

Check out on one screen.

Choose the delivery address and time, how you pay and a tip for your courier. Then place the order.

Follow it to your door.

A live status bar shows your estimated arrival, the courier's name and every update, from confirmed to delivered.

Everything between hungry and fed.

Search, categories, custom orders, live tracking and history, with an account that keeps it all together.

Search restaurants and dishes

Type a name and get matching restaurants and dishes in one list, with the match highlighted.

Menus organized by category

Main courses, starters, drinks and desserts, each one tap away. Every item shows its description and price.
