# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Portfolio project. The simulated user is a recruiter (or any hiring reviewer) who wants to place a food order: someone unfamiliar with the app, evaluating it in a few minutes, who needs to find a restaurant, pick items, and reach checkout without instruction. Not tied to a specific country or locale.

## Product Purpose
A food-ordering app. Users browse registered restaurants, open a restaurant's menu (items grouped by category such as Drinks, Starters, Desserts, Main Courses), build a basket, place the order, and track it. An account is required so the user can keep order history, build their basket, follow an order, and edit account details.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Primary navigation has four areas: main menu (home), search, order tracking, and account (history + account info).
- Accounts are mandatory (order history, basket, tracking, account editing).
- A mobile version of the design is required alongside the larger-screen version.
- Current scope: home page only, design only. No HTML deliverable for now (user withdrew it).
- Fonts named by the user: primary DIN Neuzeit Grotesk, secondary Avenir Light (neither is a free web font; substitutes are needed where they cannot be loaded).
- Locale: generic / non-country-specific; currency and copy language not fixed by the user. UI copy language chosen by the designer: English (the references are in English).

## Brand Commitments
References the user made binding, in the project folder: `Color Palette.png` (green scale 50 to 950: #F3FCF8, #E2F8F0, #C3F4E2, #94F0CE, #58EEB8, #19ECA1, #18C387, #1A996D, #197153, #134837, #0D261E), `Design Inspiration 1.png` and `Design Inspiration 2.png` (dashboard-style ordering UIs with left navigation, category picker, recommended dishes, and a right-hand order summary). No product name or logo exists yet.

## Evidence on Hand
No real restaurants, menus, photography, or logo. Nothing should be presented as real data; placeholder restaurants and dishes must be clearly illustrative.

## Product Principles
- A first-time visitor reaches "I am ordering food" within seconds, with no explanation.
- Restaurant, category, and item are the three levels of the food-ordering mental model; keep that hierarchy legible.
- The basket and order status are always one glance away.
- Generic by design: nothing that ties the product to one country or cuisine.
